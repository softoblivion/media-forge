## ⪩ Media Forge v2.0.0 ⪨

A new look: a darker window with four colour themes to choose from, one button style everywhere and a calmer motion.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Theme** — the Appearance group of the settings sheet offers four themes, Dusk, Nova, Frost and Bloom. Each one is a background colour family with its own accent for the Start button and highlights. The choice is saved and applied the next time the app opens; Nova is the default.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **Background** — darker and deeper: two soft colour fields in the theme's hue, contour lines and a faint pool of the accent along the bottom edge. It retints at once when the theme changes.
- **Buttons** — every button sits on the same bevelled plate with a light rim; the one primary action of a view (Start, Choose files, Download) stays tinted in the theme's accent. The Start pill keeps its states (Start, the progress level with the position and the speed, Done).
- **Motion** — cards drop in and leave with the same springs as the rest of the window, menus fade in and out, chevrons turn with a small overshoot; the durations are shared by every control.
